Executive summary

Everything a public program does runs on a contract. We make them work.

Meridian Advisory is a professional-services firm built on two decades of hands-on contract negotiation and operations. We negotiate and manage the agreements — vendor contracts, solicitations and participation agreements — and we run the operations that turn a signed award into something that actually delivers. We serve the organizations on the hook for a working agreement: public agencies and counties, NGOs and nonprofits, nonprofit healthcare organizations, health plans, and sports venues and events.

Our advantage is simple: the same team negotiates the contract and operates what it promises — a combination that is scarce exactly when it is needed most.

What we do.

Contract negotiation & strategy

Vendor, payer, government, and inter-agency agreements negotiated and drafted to hold up when the rules change.

Procurement & vendor management

Solicitation / RFP go-no-go and response support, SME services, and compliance for agencies and their vendors.

Network development, delegated credentialing, and NCQA / URAC readiness and oversight.

Program operations & logistics

Compliance & standards

Hands-on experience working to NCQA, URAC, and DoD standards, and to Medi-Cal / CalAIM requirements.
